---

### [0:35–1:20] DEMO SECTION 1 — Magic Design

**[Show: Canva → Create Design → Social Post → Magic Design]**

> "I'm going to create a social media post for a business coach brand. Instead of browsing templates, I click Magic Design and describe what I want: 'Bold motivational post, dark background, gold accents, minimal text, business coach audience.'"

> "I hit generate. In about five seconds — [pause for generation] — I get four options. These are full, designed layouts — not wireframes. I pick the closest one to what I want and it drops into my canvas fully editable."

> "From here I change the main text, adjust the font if needed, swap a color. I'm editing, not designing. That's the shift."

---

### [1:20–2:00] DEMO SECTION 2 — Magic Write

**[Show: text box selected → Apps → Magic Write]**

> "Now for the caption. Most people write captions manually or copy-paste into ChatGPT. Canva has Magic Write built in. I select a text element, open Magic Write, and type my prompt: 'Write a two-line motivational caption for a business coach targeting entrepreneurs.'"

> "[Show output] That's the result. I read it, I change one word, and it's done. The whole process took 15 seconds."

> "Magic Write is not going to replace a professional copywriter. But for social captions, short headlines, and filler copy? It's fast and good enough."

---

### [2:00–2:45] DEMO SECTION 3 — AI Image Generation

**[Show: Elements → AI Image Generator]**

> "The third feature is AI image generation. Instead of using stock photos, I can generate a custom image that fits my exact brand."

> "The image is unique to this design. No licensing issues, no generic stock photo vibe."
