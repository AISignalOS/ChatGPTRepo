## Demo Angle

Show how Airtable's AI features can classify, summarize, and generate content directly inside a database — without exporting data to ChatGPT. Use a content calendar base: AI categorizes topics, generates post captions, and summarizes campaign performance notes automatically.

---

## Audience Pain Point

Business owners and marketing teams use Airtable as a database but manually copy data in and out of ChatGPT for AI tasks. Airtable AI runs the AI directly in the field — no copy-paste, no context switching.

---

> "Airtable just added AI to every cell in your database. Here's what that actually means."

## Test Workflow

**[SIMULATED — replace with actual recorded steps]**

1. Open airtable.com — show the homepage
2. Open or create a content calendar base with columns: Topic, Platform, Status, Caption, Category
3. Add an AI Field: "Summarize the Topic column into a 1-line post caption for Instagram"
4. Show the AI Field generating captions for multiple rows simultaneously
5. Add a second AI Field: "Classify this topic as Educational, Promotional, or Engagement"
6. Show the classification filling in automatically for each row
7. Use AI to generate a full campaign brief from a single keyword in a record
8. Show Airtable Automations: trigger an AI action when a new record is added
