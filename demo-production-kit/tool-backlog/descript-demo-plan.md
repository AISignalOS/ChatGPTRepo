## Demo Angle

Show Descript's transcript-based video editing workflow: import a raw recording, auto-transcribe it, delete filler words and bad takes by editing the text, use AI to remove silences, and overdub a word using your own cloned voice — without re-recording.

---

## Audience Pain Point

Video creators and podcasters spend hours cutting out "um"s, pauses, and mistakes in traditional video editors. Descript makes editing as easy as editing a Word document — and it's the only tool that lets you fix spoken words by typing.

## Notes

- Import a real (or test) raw recording — do not simulate the editing on a clean clip. The messy raw footage is the story.
- Overdub requires voice training — plan time to set this up before recording, or skip it and note that it requires setup.
- Free plan: 1 hour of transcription per month — enough for demo.
- Descript vs CapCut vs Premiere AI — comparison angle for a follow-up.
- Do NOT use Overdub on anyone else's voice without explicit consent and legal review.
