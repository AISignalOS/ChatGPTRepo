## Demo Angle

Show Perplexity as a replacement for Google search in a real research workflow: ask a complex current-events or business question, show cited sources, follow up with deeper questions, and use Spaces to build a persistent research hub.

---

## Audience Pain Point

Researchers, students, and solopreneurs spend time cross-referencing multiple Google results, losing sources, and getting outdated information. They don't know there's an AI tool that searches the live web and cites every claim.

---

## Hook

> "I stopped using Google for research. Here's what I use instead."

**Alt hooks:**
- "Perplexity finds sources ChatGPT can't — here's the difference."
- "This AI answers questions with live web sources — and it's free."

## Test Workflow

**[SIMULATED — replace with actual recorded steps]**

1. Open perplexity.ai — show the search bar interface
2. Type a research question with current-event relevance: "What are the best AI tools for solopreneurs in 2026 and what do they cost?"
3. Show the result: AI-written answer with numbered citations
4. Click a citation to show it links to a real source
5. Ask a follow-up: "Compare the top 3 by free plan generosity"
