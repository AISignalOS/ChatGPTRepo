## Demo Angle

Show Notion AI as a second brain: summarize a long meeting notes page, generate a project plan from a brief, use AI search to surface buried information across your workspace, and use the AI writer to draft a client proposal inside Notion — all without leaving the app.

---

## Audience Pain Point

Notion users have built large wikis and knowledge bases they can't navigate quickly. They also spend time drafting documents manually when the AI can do it in seconds. Most don't know Notion AI is already embedded in their workspace.

---
