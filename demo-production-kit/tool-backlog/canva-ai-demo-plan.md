## Demo Angle

Show how a non-designer can create a branded social media post, a pitch deck slide, and a marketing email graphic — all with AI — in under 5 minutes, using Canva's Magic Design, Magic Write, and AI image generation tools.

---

## Audience Pain Point

Non-designers — solopreneurs, coaches, freelancers, small business owners — need professional-looking content but can't afford a designer and can't use Figma or Photoshop. They waste hours in Canva trying to make things look good manually.

---

**Alt hooks:**
- "I made a full brand kit in Canva AI without touching a single design tool."

## Test Workflow

**[SIMULATED — replace with actual recorded steps]**

1. Open canva.com — show the homepage
2. Click "Create a design" → Social Post (1080×1080)
3. Use Magic Design: type "Bold motivational post for a business coach audience, dark background, gold accents" — show generated options
4. Select one — show the editable template
5. Use Magic Write in a text box: "Write a 2-line motivational caption for a business coach" — show AI-written text
6. Switch to a new design: Presentation → Magic Design for a pitch deck slide
7. Use AI image generation: type a scene prompt, generate, insert into design
8. Export as PNG — show the final result

## What to Say

**Intro:** "Canva has had AI features for a while now, but most people don't use them — they're still dragging and dropping manually. Today I'm going to show you how to let the AI do 80% of the design work."

**On Magic Design:** "I describe what I want in plain English and Canva gives me 4–6 professional options. I pick the closest one and edit from there — that's the workflow."

**On Magic Write:** "For text elements — captions, headlines, body copy — I use Magic Write instead of typing manually. It's not perfect but it's a starting point, not a final product."

**On AI image generation:** "This is where it gets genuinely impressive. I can generate a custom image that fits my brand instead of searching stock photos."

**Verdict:** "For non-designers who need professional visual content fast — Canva AI is an A-tier tool. The free plan is generous. The paid plan adds more AI credits."
