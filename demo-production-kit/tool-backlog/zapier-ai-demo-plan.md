## Demo Angle

Show how Zapier's AI features — AI by Zapier action, Zapier Agents (chatbot builder), and plain-language Zap creation — let a non-technical solopreneur build a complete lead-capture-to-follow-up automation in under 10 minutes without writing a line of code.

Solopreneurs and small business owners know they should automate repetitive tasks but find code intimidating and existing no-code tutorials too complex. Zapier's AI now lets you describe what you want in plain English and it builds the automation for you.

## Test Workflow

**[SIMULATED — replace with actual recorded steps]**

1. Open zapier.com — show the homepage
2. Click "Create" → show the plain-English Zap builder: type "When someone fills out my Typeform, add them to a Google Sheet and send them a welcome email via Gmail"
3. Show how Zapier's AI maps the apps and steps automatically
4. Review the generated Zap — show the trigger, actions, and fields
5. Show AI by Zapier action: add a step that uses AI to write a personalized welcome email based on the form data
6. Enable and test the Zap (use a test form submission)
7. Show the result: row added to Sheet + email sent
8. Show Zapier Agents: build a simple AI chatbot that answers FAQs from a knowledge base
