## Demo Angle

Show Gemini's Google Workspace integration as its killer feature — summarize a long Gmail thread, draft a reply, pull data from a Google Doc, and create a slide summary in Slides — all from inside Gemini. This is what no other AI tool can do natively.

---

## Audience Pain Point

People who live in Google Workspace (Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides) don't realize Gemini is already embedded in their tools. They're copy-pasting into ChatGPT when the AI is already right there.

## Test Workflow

**[SIMULATED — replace with actual recorded steps]**

1. Open gemini.google.com — show the main interface

5. Use "Reply with Gemini" — show a drafted response

## What to Say

**Intro:** "Gemini is Google's AI, and its standalone version is good — but that's not why you should care. The real story is what it does when you're already inside Gmail, Docs, and Sheets."

**On Gmail integration:** "There's a Gemini button right in Gmail. I can summarize a 30-email thread in 3 seconds. That alone saves me 20 minutes a week."

**On Docs:** "Ask Gemini to improve tone, rewrite a section, or summarize the whole doc — without ever leaving Google Docs."

**Honest caveat:** "You need a Google account. Some features require Google Workspace paid plans. I'll note which is which as we go."

**Verdict:** "If your business runs on Google Workspace, Gemini is the AI upgrade that costs you nothing to try. If you're not in Google's ecosystem, ChatGPT or Claude are better starting points."
